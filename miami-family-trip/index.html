🚙 Rental Car — MIA

Mid-size SUV, MIA Rental Car Center (tram from terminal): roughly $50–70/day in August → ≈ $220–300 for Fri–Mon + taxes/fees. Budget tends cheapest (~$50), National/Alamo (~$66–68) if you want counter-skip speed.

August is family season — 3-row SUVs sell out first; book the car the same day the flights are ticketed.

🗓️ The Plan — one anchor a day, house time every day

Rule of the trip: leave the house once for something great, come back, swim, nap, repeat. Nobody's checking a schedule at 4 PM.

Friday 8/14 · Land & Splash

Anchor: nothing. That's the point.

  • 6:00 AM land → grab the SUV → Cuban breakfast (cafecito + pastelitos) in Little Havana
  • House all day — pool, beach, naps, groceries for the kitchen
  • Evening: easy stroll + dinner on Lincoln Road (open-air pedestrian mall — people-watching, gelato, no dress code)
Saturday 8/15 · Aquarium Day

Anchor: Frost Science 🦈

  • Morning: Frost Science Museum — Miami's aquarium now (Seaquarium closed for good in 2025). Three-level, 500,000-gallon aquarium you walk down through, planetarium, rooftop sharks & rays (~$30/pp, half day)
  • Lunch 10 min away at Coyo Taco in Wynwood + a quick Wynwood Walls art walk while you're there (~$12/pp)
  • Back at the house by ~3 — pool + downtime
  • Dinner: Little Havana — Versailles (the Cuban institution), then Azucar ice cream next door, live salsa on the street
Sunday 8/16 · THE Big Day (the one the Monday flight buys)

Anchor: gators or jet skis — 12-year-old's call 🐊🌊

  • Morning: Everglades airboat ride — alligators at speed, 40 min west (~$35/pp) or jet ski hour on Biscayne Bay (~$120/ski)
  • House for lunch + the hot hours — pool, hang, pack loosely
  • Late afternoon (optional, energy permitting): Aventura Mall — the unique-shopping stop that's also a kid magnet (9-story slide, arcade) — or golden hour at Crandon Park, Key Biscayne
  • Casual seafood dinner at Garcia's on the Miami River · early night — 5 AM alarm
