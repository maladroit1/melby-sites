The superposition

Eight scenarios, three axes, all held at once until the evidence forces a collapse. No probabilities are assigned yet — that's the honest state of the field, and the point of doing this.

Axis 1 · Outcome for humans
Negative case
Concentration, novel bio/cyber weapons, control regimes leak, human agency erodes ("drift"). Downside dominates.
weight: unmeasured
Base case
Uneven, messy adoption. Big winners and losers, real disruption, no clean utopia or apocalypse. Muddling through.
weight: unmeasured
Positive case
Abundance thesis: AI scientists compound discovery across physics, bio, materials; net human uplift. Star Trek, not Wall-E.
weight: unmeasured
Axis 2 · Rate of change
Slow down
Scaling hits walls (data, power, chips, regulation). Progress decelerates; timelines stretch by years.
watch: scaling-law breaks, capex pullback
Steady state
Roughly linear improvement. Diffusion-limited, not capability-limited. The economy absorbs it over decades.
watch: adoption ≈ capability curve
Speed up
Recursive self-improvement + self-scaffolding compound. Capability outruns institutions. Take-off.
watch: self-generated objectives, agent chaining
Axis 3 · The endpoints
Singularity
Superintelligence arrives; the curve goes vertical; prediction beyond the horizon becomes meaningless.
Schmidt: within ~10 yr
AI bubble
Capital and expectations outrun delivered value; a correction resets the field before the frontier claims land.
watch: revenue vs. capex gap, hype/ship ratio

These states aren't mutually exclusive — that's what makes now a quantum nightmare. You can be in "speed up + bubble" (capability real, valuations insane) or "slow down + positive" (gentle, benign diffusion) simultaneously. The series exists to measure which superpositions survive contact with evidence and which decohere.

How the model gets built

This is not punditry — it's a scorecard method.

1. Mine a source → clean transcript.  2. Extract its dated, checkable claims into a brief + prediction ledger.  3. Score quarterly — confirm / slip / refute.  4. Synthesize — once several briefs exist, roll their ledgers up and let the scenario weights above start to move. The delta between predicted and observed is the predictive signal. Synthesis of the cross-source mental model begins once the thread has enough measurements to collapse.
