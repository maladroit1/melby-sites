🐋 San Juan Island, WA — Orca Week

Five days on the Haro Strait west side · target: August 2027

USA 🇺🇸 — no passports neededPrivate waterfront houseBest from-the-house odds anywhere

Why here

The west side of San Juan Island fronts Haro Strait, where the seafloor drops to ~960 ft within 25 ft of shore — so orcas hunt salmon in the kelp line literally yards off the rocks. The stretch around Lime Kiln Point ("Whale Watch Park") is the most reliable place on the planet to see wild orcas from land. A house on this shoreline is the maximum-odds version of this trip: in good summers, pods pass roughly twice a day.

The stay

  • Primary: San Juan Waterhouse (sanjuanwaterhouse.com) — southwest side, 3 private acres with 1,400 ft of Haro Strait coastline. J, K, and L pods hunt along their shoreline and occasionally rest in the cove. Whole house, sleeps a group.
  • Backups on the same shoreline: the west-side "premier whale watching" house on VRBO (listing 50433) and other Lime Kiln–to–False Bay waterfront rentals — filter to west side · waterfront · sleeps 6+. East-side/Friday Harbor houses are nicer-per-dollar but wrong water; hold the west side.
No B&Bs. If a whole house falls through, the amenity backstop is a suite at Roche Harbor Resort (restaurants, marina, pool) and daily drives to Lime Kiln — workable, but the whole point here is whales from your own deck.

Getting there (jets + a big ferry — no small planes required)

LegDetail
FlySLC → Seattle (SEA): nonstop, ~2 hrs, hourly-ish on Delta/Alaska.
DriveRental car SEA → Anacortes: ~2 hrs. You want the car on-island; the west side is 20–30 min from the ferry landing and there's no meaningful transit.
FerryAnacortes → Friday Harbor: ~1 hr on a full-size Washington State Ferry. Reserve the car slot the moment reservations open (2 months out) — August sailings sell out. Arrive 45–60 min early.
On-islandFriday Harbor → west side: 20–30 min. Groceries: King's Market in Friday Harbor on the way.
Optional upgrade (a negotiation): Kenmore Air flies Lake Union → Friday Harbor in ~45 min — but it's a floatplane. The drive-and-ferry route is scenic and zero-drama; nothing is lost by skipping the small plane.

The five days

1

Arrive & claim the deck

Morning SLC→SEA, drive north with a lunch stop, mid-afternoon ferry, groceries in Friday Harbor, house by ~5–6pm. Evening watch from the deck — sunset over Haro Strait with the Olympics behind is the show even before whales appear. Install the alert apps tonight.
2

West-side immersion

Morning on the house shoreline (learn your kelp line — orcas hug it). Afternoon: Lime Kiln Point State Park — lighthouse, interpretive center, hydrophone speakers playing live pod audio; then San Juan Island National Historical Park (American Camp) and the bluffs at South Beach. Evening deck watch.
3

Boat day — insurance orcas

Half-day whale watch from Friday Harbor: Maya's Legacy (small boat, biologist-run) or San Juan Safaris classic tour (better for a mixed-age group). August success rates are excellent. Fish-and-chips on the harbor after.
4

Roche Harbor day

Morning: sea-kayak the west side with a guide (paddling the same water the pods use) or visit the Whale Museum in Friday Harbor. Afternoon north to Roche Harbor: sculpture park, marina stroll, oysters at Westcott Bay Shellfish, dinner at McMillin's (fine dining) or Madrona Bar & Grill (deck over the harbor, the island's only over-water sunset). Colors ceremony at dusk.
5

Last watch & home

Slow morning on the deck, checkout, midday ferry to Anacortes, drive to SEA, evening flight home. (Pad the ferry-to-flight connection by 4+ hrs.)

Stacking the orca odds

  • Orca Network (Facebook) — the fastest sighting feed in these waters; "northbound past False Bay" means 20 minutes to the deck chairs.
  • Whale Alert / WhaleReport apps — set alerts for Haro Strait.
  • Orcasound.net — live hydrophones including Lime Kiln; leave it playing in the kitchen; vocalizations often precede a visual pass.
  • 10×42 binoculars per adult; a spotting scope earns its bag space here.
  • August = peak: resident pods on Fraser-run salmon plus near-daily Bigg's transients. Even so, budget expectations: several great passes over the week, with a real shot at a kelp-line close pass.
